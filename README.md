Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-lost-sock-status-update-4b1fa3ce) bounty "Funniest Lost Sock Status Update": Entrants write one status update of 120 characters at most in the voice of a lost sock. An AI judge reads them two at a time and the funniest line takes the whole reward.

## The line

> Status update: I've moved into the dog's favourite slipper. Rent's free, but the landlord won't stop licking me.

## Run

`node main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
